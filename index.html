Farhold

An endless realm. The farther you roam, the fiercer it grows.

How to play

Roam a realm that never ends. Foes grow stronger the farther you travel from Hearthfire. Slay them for gold and gear, and equip what you find.

Villages have a market, a blacksmith who upgrades your gear, a bounty board, and a waystone for fast travel. Caves hide treasure and a guardian. Lairs hold named bosses.

Phone: drag the left side to move. Hold Attack to fight, Roll to dodge, and tap your two skill buttons.

Keyboard: WASD move, Space or click attack, Shift roll, 1 2 skills, Q potion, E interact, B bags, C character, K skills.

Red rings on the ground warn of heavy attacks. Roll out of them.
